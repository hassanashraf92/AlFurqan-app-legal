1. Introduction

Welcome to SocialFlow. This Privacy Policy describes how SocialFlow ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application (the "App") and related services (collectively, the "Services").

By using SocialFlow, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our Services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address, password, name, and profile photo (optional).
  • Business Information: Business name, industry, and other profile details you choose to provide.
  • Social Media Accounts: When you connect your social media accounts (Facebook, Instagram, X/Twitter, LinkedIn), we receive authorization tokens that allow us to post on your behalf. We do not store your social media passwords.
  • Content: Posts, images, captions, and other content you create or upload through the App.
  • Communications: When you contact us for support or feedback, we collect the information you provide in those communications.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use our Services, we automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
  • Usage Information: Features you use, actions you take, time spent in the App, and frequency of use.
  • Log Information: IP address, access times, pages viewed, and the page you visited before navigating to our Services.
  • Analytics Data: We use Firebase Analytics to understand how you interact with our Services.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

We receive information from third-party services you connect to SocialFlow:

  • Social Media Platforms: Profile information, audience demographics, and post performance data from connected accounts (Facebook, Instagram, X/Twitter, LinkedIn).
  • Authentication Providers: Basic profile information when you sign in with Apple or Google.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and Improve Services: Create and manage your account, enable social media posting, generate AI content, and improve our features.
  • Personalization: Customize your experience, remember your preferences, and provide relevant content suggestions.
  • AI Features: Process your content through AI services to generate captions, images, and content ideas.
  • Analytics: Analyze post performance, track engagement metrics, and provide insights.
  • Communications: Send you notifications about scheduled posts, account activity, and service updates.
  • Support: Respond to your requests, questions, and feedback.
  • Security: Detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, and violations of our Terms of Service.
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

We do not sell your personal information. We share your information only in the following circumstances:

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Data Storage

Your information is stored securely using Firebase services, which employ industry-standard security measures. We use encryption for data in transit and at rest.

5.2 Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit using HTTPS/TLS
  • Encryption of sensitive data at rest

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you Services. When you delete your account, we delete your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal, regulatory, or security purposes.
