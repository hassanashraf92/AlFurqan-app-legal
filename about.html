About Us

Last Updated: February 15, 2026

Athar is a community Quran reading platform built to make Khatma (collective Quran completion) simple, organized, and spiritually uplifting.

Our goal is to help friends, families, and communities come together around the Quran—by coordinating reading parts (Juz), tracking progress, and celebrating completion as a group.

Our Focus: A respectful, distraction-free experience — no ads, no tracking analytics, and no selling of personal data.

1. Our Mission

We believe Quran reading is more meaningful when it’s shared. Athar exists to support:

  • Building consistent Quran reading habits
  • Enabling group Khatma with clear organization and shared accountability
  • Strengthening community bonds through worship and collaboration
  • Keeping the experience clean, respectful, and easy to use

2. What Athar Helps You Do

2.1 Create & Join Khatma Groups

  • Create a Khatma group in seconds
  • Invite others using a simple join code
  • Manage participants and progress in one place

2.2 Claim & Complete Reading Parts

  • Claim a Juz (part) inside your group
  • Mark it as completed when done
  • See group progress clearly and transparently

2.3 Stay Updated

  • Get notifications when important group activity happens (e.g., new participants, completed parts)
  • Stay aligned with the group without constant messaging

3. Our Community Values

Athar is designed around respectful Quranic etiquette and a welcoming environment. We expect all users to:

  • Be respectful and kind to others
  • Avoid spam, harassment, or disruptive behavior
  • Use appropriate names and profile photos
  • Support a positive, spiritually-focused community experience

To protect the community, we may limit access or suspend accounts that violate these expectations.

4. Transparency & Trust

We take trust seriously. Our design choices prioritize simplicity and privacy:

  • No advertising
  • No behavioral tracking or analytics
  • No selling of personal information
  • Only the data required to operate the Service is stored securely

For details about data handling and storage, please review our Privacy Policy.
